Start with an empty list A and a counter n = 0. Apply these operations in order and report the final A and n.

1. Append 4 to A.
2. Append 7 to A.
3. Increase n by the length of A.
4. Insert 1 at the front of A.
5. Remove the largest element from A.
6. Append n to A.
7. Reverse A.
8. Set n to the sum of A.
9. Remove the first occurrence of 4 from A.
10. Append (n minus the last element of A) to A.
11. If A has an odd number of elements, append 0; otherwise remove the last element.
12. Swap the first and last elements of A.
13. Increase n by the number of even elements in A (treat 0 as even).
14. Remove every element greater than n/2.
15. Append n to A.
